The shift handover

From RFQ to final sign-off.

A five-stage process that keeps every project on spec, on budget and on the original schedule.

1

Scope & estimate

We review your drawings, specs and site conditions, then return a fixed-price proposal within two weeks.

2

Engineering & detailing

Our in-house engineering team produces full shop drawings, weld maps and QA/QC plans.

3

Fabrication

In-shop fabrication under controlled conditions, with third-party NDT at every critical hold point.

4

Delivery & erection

Fleet of 14 flatbeds, four cranes and a field crew that has set steel on sites from Louisiana to Alberta.

5

Handover & closeout

As-built documentation, O&M manuals, training and a full warranty — no lingering punch-list.
