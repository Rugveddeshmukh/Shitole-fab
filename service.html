How we deliver

From drawing to delivery.

01

Spec & estimate

You send us the drawings or scope. We review, price and return a fixed-fee proposal within 10 business days.

02

Engineering & detailing

Our in-house team produces shop drawings, lift plans and weld maps. Every deliverable is peer-reviewed before release.

03

Fabrication

CNC cutting, robotic welding, manual TIG and stick, plus full NDT at every hold point. ASME Section VIII and API 1104 compliant.

04

Delivery & installation

We load, truck and rig on your schedule. Our field crews handle site prep, setting, alignment and final bolting.

05

Commissioning & support

Pressure tests, system checks, documentation handover and ongoing maintenance. We stay on site until the line is live.
