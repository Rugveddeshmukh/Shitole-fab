Our story

The shop that kept building.

Established in 2010, Shitole Fabweld Engineers has established itself as a trusted manufacturer of industrial products such as main gates, roofing structures, furniture, sheet metal fabrication, water tankers and containers.

Our products are specially designed to deliver reliable performance and meet customer expectations. We also provide customized fabrication solutions tailored to specific client requirements, ensuring the right solutions for every project.

Our commitment to superior product quality and precision workmanship has helped us build lasting relationships with our clients. We continuously implement quality control measures to maintain accuracy, improve our manufacturing processes and meet evolving industry requirements.
