Our story

The shop that kept building.

Ironbound Industrial started as a three-man welding shop on the Gulf Coast. The first job was a stairway for a chemical plant. The second was a pressure vessel. By the fifth year, we were the ones the refineries called when a turnaround needed to happen on schedule.

Today, we operate across three states — Texas, Louisiana and Oklahoma — with a 140,000 sq ft fabrication facility, a full engineering team and a safety record that made us an API Q1 and ASME Section VIII certified shop.

We still answer the phone at 2 am when a line goes down. That part hasn't changed.
